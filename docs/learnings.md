Search findings, not reviewed content. Only titles and short descriptions were seen; no video was watched, so accuracy and teaching quality are unverified. Found 2026-11-07. Check each video's examples against the meter table the app seeds before relying on it.

## Start here: basics (laghu/guru syllables, ganas, scanning a verse)
- [Chandas – Introduction to Sanskrit Prosody (playlist)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLmozlYyYE-ERI52EVhtqsdpTUBKf7IoAd): structured introduction; best starting point.
- [A Quick Guide On Poetic Metre](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gdfw33Husos): how to work out the metre of any verse, using anuṣṭubh (the Gītā metre) as the example. A [second video with the same title](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WbWYb5P9KOo) exists.
- [Understanding the Grammar of the Chhandas](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tClM06N-uqg)
- [Sanskrit prosody / Chhand](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I1MODLGE3Ao): description says it comes from NCERT material.

## The main metres
- [Anuṣṭubh and Triṣṭubh variations (Dr. Vasantalakshmi)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ITPP8CCPk8U): the two metres most common in epic verses.
- [SanskritMetres 12: Jagatī and stotra metres](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ffbqy_W76Co): part of a numbered series; earlier parts may cover other metres.
- [Chandas – The Vedic Metres (Dr. Shreehari Gokarnakar)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7BNkWHUXcds)
